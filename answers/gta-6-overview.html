Tell me about GTA 6

An overview of Grand Theft Auto VI from Rockstar Games, as of its launch day, 19 November 2026.

Short answer: Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar Games' new open-world crime game and the first new mainline GTA since GTA V in 2013. It comes out today, 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is set in the fictional state of Leonida, Rockstar's version of Florida, which includes a modern Vice City. You play two leads, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Critics have given it some of the highest scores ever recorded. There is no PC version yet, and GTA Online for GTA 6 has no date yet.
Note on freshness: the game launched on the day this page was written, so review averages, sales numbers and the PC/Online plans are changing fast. Figures marked "reported" come from press and analyst estimates, not from Rockstar.

Story and setting

GTA 6 brings the series back to Vice City, the Miami-inspired city last seen as a main setting in GTA: Vice City (2002, set in 1986). This time the city sits inside a whole fictional state, Leonida, with cities, wetlands, beaches and small towns modelled on Florida. In the trailers this includes the Everglades-like "Grassrivers" and the Keys-like "Leonida Keys".

This is the first mainline GTA with a female lead. Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval are often compared to Bonnie and Clyde. A score that should have been easy goes wrong, and the pair end up in a criminal conspiracy that spreads across Leonida. Like the trailers, the game satirises modern American life: social media, viral videos and news coverage.

How it got here: development timeline

  1. September 2013: GTA V launches. It goes on to sell more than 200 million copies, so expectations for a sequel are huge.
  2. February 2022: Rockstar confirms that work on the next GTA is "well underway".
  3. September 2022: a large leak of early development footage. Rockstar confirms the footage is real.
  4. December 2023: Trailer 1 is released and confirms Vice City, Lucia and a 2025 window. It breaks YouTube view records for a non-music video in its first 24 hours.
  5. May 2025: first delay, to 26 May 2026. Trailer 2 introduces Jason and more of Leonida.
  6. 6 November 2025: second delay, to 19 November 2026. Rockstar says it needs the extra time for polish.
  7. 25 June 2026: pre-orders open.
  8. Autumn 2026: an "Extended Look" gameplay video (late August) and the launch trailer (mid-October).
  9. ~10 November 2026: digital pre-load starts for pre-orders. Sources disagree on the exact day.
  10. 18 November 2026: the review embargo lifts.
  11. 19 November 2026: worldwide console launch.

Reception

Reviews went live on 18 November 2026 and are close to universally positive:

  • Metacritic: 99 on PS5 from roughly 150 critic reviews. That ties the all-time record held by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998). For comparison, GTA IV scored 98 and GTA V 97.
  • OpenCritic: 99 average from top critics (reported).
  • IGN gave it 10/10. Reviewer Luke Reilly called it "the single most impressive video game I have ever played". GameSpot and Game Informer reportedly also gave 10/10.
  • Common criticisms: console versions run at 30 fps with no 60 fps performance mode at launch, slow pacing in places, and some fussy systems.

Aggregate scores move in the first days after launch, so check Metacritic or OpenCritic for current numbers.

Commercial expectations

  • Sensor Tower estimates more than $1.65 billion in pre-order sales, about three quarters of it on PS5 (reported).
  • Analysts call it a candidate for the biggest entertainment launch ever. Wells Fargo reportedly raised its forecast to about 65 million copies sold by the end of March 2027.
  • The launch is also an event outside games. YouTube is running a 24-hour launch livestream, and an original score album is due in December.

PC version

Rockstar has not announced a PC version or PC release date. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said that "virtually all of our hit titles end up on all [platforms] over time" and that any announcement will come from Rockstar. GamesBeat reported in October 2026, citing unnamed sources, that a PC release is planned for early 2027. This has not been confirmed. Past Rockstar games took a while to reach PC: GTA V about 19 months, Red Dead Redemption 2 about 13 months.

GTA Online

GTA 6 launches as a single-player game. Rockstar is widely expected to follow it with an online mode, as GTA Online did for GTA V (launched two weeks after the game in 2013), but has not announced a date or details yet.

Should you play it?

  • Yes, if you own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, enjoy open-world, story-driven crime games, and don't mind 30 fps.
  • Wait, if you only play on PC, or you mainly want online multiplayer. Neither is available yet.
  • Make sure you have about 212 GB of free storage.
