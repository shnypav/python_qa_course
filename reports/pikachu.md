## Description

Pikachu is a small Electric-type rodent that first appeared in Generation I. It stores electricity in pouches in its cheeks and lets it out when it is angry or in danger. Some Pokédex entries say that a group of Pikachu can build up enough electricity to cause lightning storms. It is the best-known Pokémon and the franchise's mascot.

> "When it is angered, it immediately discharges the energy stored in the pouches in its cheeks." — *Pokémon Scarlet*

> "It keeps its tail raised to monitor its surroundings. If you yank its tail, it will try to bite you." — *Pokémon Yellow*

## Evolution

```
Pichu ──(level up with high friendship)──▶ Pikachu ──(Thunder Stone)──▶ Raichu
                                                  └─(Thunder Stone, in Alola)─▶ Alolan Raichu (Electric/Psychic)
```

- **Pichu → Pikachu:** level up with high friendship (220 or more).
- **Pikachu → Raichu:** use a Thunder Stone. In the Alola games (Sun/Moon, Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon), the stone gives Alolan Raichu instead.

**Other forms:** Gigantamax Pikachu (Sword/Shield), the Cap Pikachu (Original, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Partner, World), Cosplay Pikachu with its Rock Star, Belle, Pop Star, Ph.D. and Libre outfits (Omega Ruby/Alpha Sapphire), and the Partner Pikachu from Let's Go. That is 16 alternate forms in total.
